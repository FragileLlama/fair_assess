Are you missing a property tax exemption?

Exemptions lower your bill every year, and if you qualified in past years but didn't receive one, you can apply for refunds for up to 3 prior years through a "Certificate of Error."

Answer these questions:

  1. Do you live in the home as your main residence? You likely qualify for the Homeowner Exemption. Once granted it renews automatically, but it's commonly missing after a purchase or refinance.
  2. Are you (or your spouse) 65 or older? Look at the Senior Exemption. Many seniors who had it in the past stopped receiving it because they didn't reapply.
  3. Are you 65+ with a total household income at or below the state limit? The Senior Assessment Freeze can hold your assessed value steady. It requires an application each year.
  4. Do you have a disability, or are you a veteran with a disability? There are separate exemptions for persons with disabilities and for veterans with disabilities.
  5. Did you recently return from active military duty? See the Returning Veterans' Exemption.
  6. Have you lived in your home 10+ years in an area with rapidly rising values? Ask about the Long-time Occupant Homeowner Exemption.

Check which exemptions appear on your most recent second-installment tax bill. If one you qualify for is missing, apply on the Cook County Assessor's website (cookcountyassessoril.gov → Exemptions). Applying is free.

Eligibility rules, income limits and deadlines change; confirm them on the Assessor's site. {{ BRAND }} isn't affiliated with Cook County.
