How to file (free, about 20 minutes)

{% if window.state == "open" %}

Deadline: {{ window.window.closes.strftime("%A, %B %-d, %Y") }} for {{ fr.township }} Township at the Board of Review.

{% endif %}
  1. Save this packet as a PDF (Print → Save as PDF).
  2. Go to the Cook County Board of Review website, cookcountyboardofreview.com, and create a free account. Filing is free.
  3. Start a new residential appeal for PIN {{ format_pin(fr.pin) }} during your township's filing window. Choose the reason that matches your evidence: lack of uniformity (comparables) and/or overvaluation (recent purchase or sales).
  4. Enter at least 3–5 comparable PINs from the table above. Pick the most similar ones that are assessed lower than yours.
  5. Upload this PDF as supporting evidence. Add photos of any condition problems (foundation, roof, water damage) and, if you bought recently, your closing statement.
  6. Submit, and keep the confirmation number. The Board mails a written decision.
  7. If the Board doesn't lower your value, you can appeal to the state Property Tax Appeal Board (PTAB), usually within 30 days of the Board's decision. Check the date on the decision letter.
