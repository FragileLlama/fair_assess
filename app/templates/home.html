How appeals work in Cook County

  1. Assessor appeal: after your township's reassessment notice, you have about 30 days to appeal to the Assessor.
  2. Board of Review appeal: the second level. Each township gets a filing window between summer and December. Your free report shows how often appeals in your township actually won.
  3. State Property Tax Appeal Board (PTAB): if the Board of Review says no, you can go one level further.

You can appeal every year, even when your township isn't being reassessed.
